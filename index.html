Guanqun Cao received the Ph.D. degree in Signal Processing and Computing Science from Tampere University, Finland, in 2018. He subsequently worked with Volvo Cars, where he was a Senior Data Scientist and Deep Learning Engineer from 2018 to 2021. Since 2022, he has been with the European R&D organization of Geely, where he is currently a Data Architect and Senior Machine Learning Engineer with Geely Technology Europe. His research interests include machine learning, deep learning, data mining, computer vision, and intelligent automotive systems.
